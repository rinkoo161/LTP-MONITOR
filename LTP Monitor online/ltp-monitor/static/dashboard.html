⚠ Regression / Stress Testing - worst-case single-candle shocks, correlated crashes, IV crush

Simulates adverse scenarios against the REAL stoploss/spread-exit logic — including gap slippage (price jumps past the stop within one candle, a real risk no threshold-based stop fully prevents) and a correlated shock across all 4 indices at once.

Daily Trading Journal - day-wise outcomes in plain language

Shadow-Signal Journal - every signal, approved or rejected, and whether the risk agent was right

Every rejected signal is tracked forward against real subsequent prices for up to 90 minutes: did it hit target (rejection may have cost a win) or stoploss (rejection was correct)? This is how we measure whether the risk gates are actually adding value, not just guessing.
